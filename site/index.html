Straight answers

Asked on the Coast.

Who actually shows up to do the work?
Depending on the job: a vetted Coast handyman from our roster, or a licensed contractor when the law or the work calls for it. The desk stays your single point of contact either way — you never chase anyone.
What if it's a gas or electrical job?
Then it's a licensed-contractor job by law (Technical Safety BC), and we treat it that way from the first callback — booked with a licensed, insured pro, permits where required. If your home has a suite or is attached (duplex, townhouse, strata), most electrical needs a licensed contractor, full stop.
How fast is "same business day," really?
Call or submit before mid-afternoon and you hear from us that day. Evenings and weekends, you hear back the next business day. Either way, you get a straight answer — never voicemail into the void.
Why can't you give me one exact price on the phone?
Because honest pricing from photos is a band — a range with its assumptions stated. Anyone quoting an exact number for a job they haven't seen is guessing, and you'll pay for the guess. We give the range, then confirm on site before work starts.
What does "batching" mean for me?
Your job joins a run with neighbours' jobs — a Tuesday in Sechelt, a Thursday in Gibsons. That efficiency is exactly why tradespeople take our small jobs, and it's how the desk keeps the callback and the estimate free.
Where do you cover?
Langdale to Pender Harbour — Gibsons, Roberts Creek, Davis Bay, Wilson Creek, Sechelt, Sandy Hook, Halfmoon Bay, Madeira Park. Outside that, call anyway; if we can't do it fast, we'll say so on the callback instead of not calling at all.
