Straight answers

Asked on the Coast.

You'll really help me fix it myself, for free?
Yes — when it's a safe, simple fix (aerator, flapper, struck latch, tripped GFCI, caulking). The chat gives you the steps; if any step feels past you, say so and it becomes a booking. We figure the goodwill is worth more than the $95 job, and seniors on the Coast talk.
Who actually shows up to do the work?
A vetted Coast handyman from the roster for handyman-lane jobs, or a licensed contractor when the law or the work calls for it. The desk stays your single point of contact either way — you never chase anyone.
What if it's a gas or electrical job?
Licensed-contractor job by law (Technical Safety BC), booked with a licensed, insured pro, permits where required. If your home has a suite or is attached (duplex, townhouse, strata), most electrical needs a licensed contractor, full stop. We don't coach regulated DIY.
How fast is "same business day," really?
Call or chat before mid-afternoon and you hear from us that day. Evenings and weekends, the next business day. Either way: a straight answer, never voicemail into the void.
Why a price band instead of one exact number?
Honest pricing from photos is a range with assumptions stated. Anyone quoting exact sight-unseen is guessing, and you'll pay for the guess. You get the band first, confirmed on site before work starts.
What does "batching" mean for me?
Your job joins a run with neighbours' jobs — Tuesday in Sechelt, Thursday in Gibsons. That efficiency is the whole reason tradespeople take our small jobs, and why diagnosis stays free.
Where do you cover?
Langdale to Pender Harbour — Gibsons, Roberts Creek, Davis Bay, Wilson Creek, Sechelt, Sandy Hook, Halfmoon Bay, Madeira Park. Outside that, call anyway; if we can't do it fast, we'll say so instead of not calling back.
