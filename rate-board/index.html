How the numbers get real

From research draft to rate cards

Supply test — 5 trade conversations

This board is the prop. Every call asks "is this right for the Coast?" — each correction is an easy yes for the trade, and the ask feels like research, not recruitment.

Rate cards — one per trade

The 9-field tailgate sheet (rates by job type, markup %, call-out, permits, job templates, hours, radius, capacity). Desk prices from the card only, never on its own.

Delta log — estimate vs final

Every completed job logs band-given vs invoiced. Quarterly review refreshes the board so bands never go stale (stale bands burn trust).

v1.0 — corrected, then public

Research tags retire; bands stand on trade cards. Public copy stays trade-generic, "not a quote," no named businesses. Goes live on wereturncalls.ca on Sam's go.
